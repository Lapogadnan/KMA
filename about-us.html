Kill My Assignments
A focus-first study companion built for students who need more than just a timer.
What we built & why
Distraction is the number one enemy of student productivity. We set out to build something that fights back.
The Problem
Students with attention disorders struggle more than neurotypical students to lock in during exam time. These students deserve the same opportunities to succeed.
The Solution
Kill My Assignments adds random check-in popups that you must click or all your progress is lost!. You can't fake presence. Every completed cycle earns points, so studying becomes a game you want to win.
